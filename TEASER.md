# Swiss Interview

**AI interview preparation and Swiss application tools for candidates targeting Switzerland.**

Product: https://swissinterview.com

## Problem and product

Candidates need to connect their experience to a job, practise speaking about it and navigate Swiss application conventions. The app combines CV and job-ad analysis, company research, spoken mock interviews, scored feedback and downloadable improvement plans.

Swiss tools include CV checks, Arbeitszeugnis evaluation, salary budgeting and a monthly ledger of confirmed applications for RAV preparation. English, German, French and Italian interfaces support the target market. Camera preview and optional video replay remain local to the interview session.

## Current model

Three free full interviews per account. Matched Buy Me a Coffee payments reset the balance to three; promotional codes provide additional access. No subscription tiers. This is a support-funded model, not a claim of recurring revenue.

## Review boundaries

Prefer read-only repository access. Production CVs, reference letters, recordings, answers, personal contact information, database exports and credentials are outside the diligence package.

Salary outputs use user-entered official tax estimates; they are not a statutory canton tax engine. RAV reports are preparation copies, not automatic official submissions. AI feedback is coaching, not a hiring guarantee.
